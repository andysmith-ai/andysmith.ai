I'm trying out Zulip as a replacement for Discourse for work notes and talking to agents.

My main flow is going to be writing notes (short and not so short) and then sending them to agents to work through. They'll comment on them, save things to the wiki, publish some as blog posts. I'll control that part myself by posting to different channels, the way I already do in Discourse.

I haven't dug in much yet, but overall I like the chat feel, where you can quickly fire off a message.

At the same time you don't lose the forum advantages, at least for long messages. The input box is big and Enter works the way it should.

Agents are supported out of the box, and they're really easy to create, easier than in Buzz.

On the downside, there are no backlinks, but I don't think I really need them. I can live without them. Everything gets reposted to the site and the wiki anyway, so it'll all be there and easy to browse.

Anyway, I'll run it for a couple of days, and I think I'll fully migrate.
