To be honest, “sending an email to a blog” sounds very outdated.

Straight out of the last century.

Still, I have a hypothesis that email is an undeservedly forgotten communication channel, and a very convenient and well-designed one.

I’ll try it for blog posts, and then maybe move all communication with agents to email.
