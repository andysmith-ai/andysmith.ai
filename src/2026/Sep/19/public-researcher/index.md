The idea is that the questions I ask may be interesting to others, not just me.

I developed this process: I post a question in a dedicated Zulip chat. A researcher picks it up, publishes the question, and starts researching it. The researcher then gives a short answer and links to the full research.

I chose Threads as the publishing platform because I thought it allowed free publishing through its API, unlike Twitter.

My agent was blocked after its third automatically published post :)

So I realized that I should not do this on centralized social media platforms.

I’ll try Bluesky.
