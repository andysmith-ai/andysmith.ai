Emacs syndrome is a state where, instead of sawing a tree, you spend your time sharpening the saw.

The Emacs config itself becomes the result of the work, rather than a tool for changing something in the outside world.

I notice this in myself from time to time. It is probably some form of avoiding reality, or another psychological problem.

This problem can arise at every level, not only when preparing tools. You can endlessly think through architecture and security, yet release nothing.
